Permitting

Permit sequencing and follow-through with the agencies that have to sign off, as part of the job.

In Tampa Bay, permitting is often the critical path. Construction management includes who needs which stamp, in which order, and what the drawings still owe the reviewer. We treat permits as hold points in the same roadmap as land development, buyout, and the field, not as a courier errand running beside the job.

The work is sequencing and follow-through: intake, completeness, comments, resubmittal, and the inspections that the permit then requires. A commercial site, a multi-family building, and a custom house do not share the same agency path. We stay on the owner's side of that process so the schedule is honest about what has not yet been approved.

This page lives under Construction Management because permitting is part of running the job from concept through completion, or of stepping in when a stall is a permit stall. Consulting can include a permitting reading before anyone files. Project management includes inspection readiness once the framework already exists. A parallel page under Industries describes the same work without listing permitting as a primary market.
