Construction management for land development and construction

Coordination of the work in the field — trades, schedule, and site — for owners who need the job run, not only advised.

Construction management is the field practice. Where consulting advises and project management holds scope, cost, and timeline, construction management is the on-site coordination that keeps trades, deliveries, and inspections from colliding.

We work as the owner’s construction manager: one contact for the superintendent-level questions, the daily sequence, and the site constraints that never show up cleanly on a drawing. That includes new construction, renovation, demolition, and the site work that comes with land development.

Construction management is a primary service, equal in the practice to consulting and project management. It is not a sub-bullet under consulting. Call (813) 323-4648 if the job already needs someone on the site, not another memo.
