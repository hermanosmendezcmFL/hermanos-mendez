Construction management

Coordination of the work in the field — trades, schedule, and site — for construction projects in Tampa Bay.

Construction management is how we keep the job moving on site. Hermanos Mendez Construction Management coordinates trades, schedule, and the site so owners have one accountable partner in the field.

It pairs with consulting when a project still needs planning, and with project management for scope, cost, and timeline across the whole job — including land development work that becomes construction.
