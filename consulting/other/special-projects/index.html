Special projects consulting

Consulting for scopes with an odd constraint: a shutdown window, an occupied site, or a study before anyone bids.

Special projects are jobs with an odd constraint: a tight shutdown window, a site that has to stay in use, a mix of demolition and occupied renovation, or an owner who needs a study before anyone bids.

Consulting here is about defining the constraint, the sequence, and who has to say yes (permits, inspections, utilities) before the field is asked to perform.
