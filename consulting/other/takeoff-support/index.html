Takeoff Support and Contract Negotiations

Quantity takeoff support, and help reading the commercial terms of a construction contract before you sign. We are not a law firm.

Takeoff support means working from the drawings and specifications to organize quantities so an owner or contractor can bid, budget, or buy against a shared list. The value is not only the numbers; it is that everyone is finally counting the same job. Most bid spreads that look alarming turn out to be three contractors pricing three different scopes, and a takeoff is how that gets exposed before an award rather than after.

We also flag what the drawings do not say. Missing details, allowances doing too much work, and scope that lands in the gap between two trades are the items that become change orders. Naming them during takeoff is far cheaper than discovering them in the field. This is consulting support, not a guarantee of another estimator's number.

Contract negotiations, on this page, means sitting with the commercial terms that actually affect how the job runs: allowances and alternates, schedule and delay language, change-order mechanics, retainage, payment timing, and what complete will mean at closeout. Owners routinely sign documents that price the work correctly and describe the process badly. We help you see that before signature.

We do not provide legal advice and we do not replace your attorney. Bring the contract to counsel for legal review; bring it to us for the construction reading. Takeoff support is used on both commercial and residential engagements, and pairs naturally with purchasing assistance during buyout.
