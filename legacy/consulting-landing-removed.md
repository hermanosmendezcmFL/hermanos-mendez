# Consulting landing copy removed 28 August 2026

Ryan’s Word brief asked that everything below come off the Consulting landing page (`/consulting/` and `/es/consultoria/`). It is banked here verbatim (English and Spanish) so it can be reused on other pages.

Source of truth at removal: `tools/pages.json` (page `en_path` `/consulting/`). The same strings were rendered by `tools/build.py` (`interior_body()`). Global nav/footer generators in `tools/chrome.py` were not stripped except where the brief renamed Consulting dropdown labels; footer links that still resolve were left in place.

The Consulting landing after this change is: header, banner (new H1 + lead), new body, Partner Solutions sidebar, contact CTA, footer. The lower Related / More card bar is gone.

## 3. Body paragraphs

**Where it lived:** Consulting landing `.prose` in the first content section on `/consulting/` and `/es/consultoria/`.

### English

Consulting at HMCM is how owners hire us for judgment, scoping, and planning. It is the advisory side of land development and construction in Tampa Bay.

Typical work starts with a site or a set of drawings. We identify what has to happen for the job to be permittable and buildable, then put that into a sequence an owner can act on.

When the engagement grows into field coordination or full cost-and-schedule control, we move into construction management or project management.

### Spanish

La consultoría en HMCM es cómo nos contratan para criterio, alcance y planificación. Es el lado de asesoría del desarrollo de terrenos y la construcción en Tampa Bay.

El trabajo típico empieza con un predio o un juego de planos. Identificamos lo que hace falta para que la obra sea permisible y construible, y lo dejamos en una secuencia que el propietario pueda ejecutar.

Si el encargo crece hacia coordinación de campo o control de costo y plazo, pasamos a gerencia de construcción o de proyectos.
